Four new voicepacks. The fourteen 1.0.1 packs are unchanged and included again
with the same files and checksums, so existing installations need no update.

- **Ajantis 2.0.0** (26 slots): his original BG1 companion voice only.
- **Viconia (BG1) 1.0.0** (27 slots) and **Viconia (BG2) 1.0.0** (39 slots):
  her original BG1 and BG2/Throne of Bhaal companion voices, each line in its
  own event slot.
- **Garithos 1.1.0** (23 slots): IAIWE's Lord Garithos soundset, with
  recordings from Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne (Blizzard Entertainment),
  converted into an EE installer. The soundset and the two included portraits
  are IAIWE's work.

Every pack installs on BG:EE, BG:EE with Siege of Dragonspear, BG2:EE, and EET
(EE 2.6 soundset layout or a compatible later version). Download the ZIP for
the voice you want, extract it into the game directory, and run its setup
executable. The recordings and subtitles are English. To replace an earlier
build of the same pack, close the game and choose Reinstall in WeiDU; WeiDU may
also reinstall mods installed after that pack.

Validation on BG:EE/BG2:EE 2.7.3.0 tables: each new pack passed eight
game/language fixtures with reinstall and uninstall restoration and rejected
six invalid targets. Ajantis and Viconia audio decodes exactly to the original
game recordings; Garithos keeps IAIWE's WAVs byte for byte. The fourteen 1.0.1
archives were rebuilt byte-identically and passed their installer tests again.
Live listening/playtesting remains pending.
